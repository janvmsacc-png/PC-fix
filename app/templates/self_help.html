SAFETY CHECK

Stop before you start if you notice:

  • Smoke, sparks, burning smell, or unusual crackling
  • Spilled liquid or a wet device
  • A swollen battery, cracked case, or exposed wiring
  • Severe heat that makes the device unsafe to touch

If any apply, unplug the device only if it is safe to do so. Do not open it or keep trying to power it on.
